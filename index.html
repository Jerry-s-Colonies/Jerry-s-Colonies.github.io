Life on The Frontier

Start Something From Nothing
Ever pictured yourself carving a patch of land into a country people actually want to live in? Grab some friends, plant a flag, and see where it goes.
Choose Your Neighbors Wisely
Some of the best stories here start with two strangers agreeing not to fight each other. Where that friendship goes next is entirely up to you.
Not Every Story Ends in Peace
When talks break down, the only thing left to decide is who's still standing. How far you're willing to go is a question only you can answer.

There's no rulebook telling you how to live here — just the players around you, and whatever order you all decide to keep.

About

A Community-Run Frontier

The Frontier keeps rules to a minimum because most order comes from the players themselves — through trade, diplomacy, and the occasional war. It's a community-driven experience that's growing every day, and there's always room for another builder, trader, or leader to leave their mark.
